Statement About Us

Dignity, Economic Empowerment & Independence for Vulnerable Single Mothers

Rise to Empower Single Mothers Africa (RESMA) is a Ugandan non-profit organization established in 2025 to advance the rights, dignity, resilience, and socio-economic empowerment of vulnerable single mothers and their children. Based in Fort Portal Tourism City, RESMA brings together dedicated professionals and community leaders committed to addressing the structural challenges that perpetuate poverty, gender inequality, social exclusion, and vulnerability.

Our work is grounded in the belief that when a mother is empowered, an entire family has the opportunity to thrive. Through holistic, community-driven interventions, RESMA promotes economic empowerment and sustainable livelihoods, access to health and social services, child protection and education, human rights, and community resilience. We equip mothers with practical skills, livelihood opportunities, business support, and knowledge while creating pathways for their children to remain in school, stay healthy, and build a secure future. Our work contributes in advancing UN sustainable Development Goals.

RESMA goes beyond short-term assistance by addressing the root causes of vulnerability and strengthening the capacity of families to become self-reliant. We work alongside communities, local institutions, development partners, and other stakeholders to create practical and lasting solutions that restore dignity, expand opportunity, and enable vulnerable families to move from dependency toward independence.

👁️ Our Vision

A defined, empowered, and self-sustaining life for all vulnerable single mothers and their children in Africa.

🎯 Our Mission

To holistically empower single mothers and their children through sustainable, inclusive, and community-driven interventions that promote social justice, economic resilience, and well-being.

Our Core Values

1. Integrity

We uphold honesty and strong moral principles in all our actions and decisions.

2. Gender Equality

We believe in equal rights and opportunities for women, challenging discrimination at every level.

3. Respect for Human Rights

We advocate for and protect the fundamental rights of every mother and child we serve.

4. Transparency & Accountability

We are open about our operations and accountable to our beneficiaries, partners, and the public.

5. Empathy & Compassion

We listen, understand, and act with deep care for the struggles faced by single mothers.

6. Community Participation

We ensure that our interventions are driven by the communities we aim to support.

7. Inclusiveness

We embrace diversity and ensure no mother or child is left behind due to background or circumstance.

Our Strategic Objectives

  • To economically empower vulnerable single mothers through vocational, entrepreneurship, and income-generating skills training.
  • To support children's education by providing school fees and scholastic materials for every child to acquire formal education.
  • To improve access to healthcare, counseling and guidance, and social education services.
  • To advocate for the rights and dignity of single mothers and children through legal aid, civic education, and public engagement.
  • To reduce stigma and promote social reintegration of single mothers into productive community life.
  • To strengthen the leadership, self-esteem, and life skills of single mothers.
  • To establish long-term partnerships that promote sustainable, generational social impact.

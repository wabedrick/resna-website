Who We Are

Dignity, Empowerment & Independence for Single Mothers

Rise to Empower Single Mothers Africa (RESMA) was established to champion the fundamental rights, socio-economic resilience, and maternal dignity of vulnerable single mothers and their children.

We operate from Fort Portal Tourism City, Uganda, addressing root causes of poverty, gender inequality, and social exclusion through holistic community development.

🌍 UN Sustainable Development Goals Framework

SDG 1: No Poverty SDG 3: Good Health SDG 4: Quality Education SDG 5: Gender Equality

👁️ Our Vision

A dignified, empowered, and self-sustaining life for all vulnerable single mothers and their children in Africa.

🎯 Our Mission

To holistically empower single mothers and their children through sustainable, inclusive, and community-driven interventions that promote social justice, economic resilience, and well-being.

Our Core Values

1. Integrity

We uphold honesty and strong moral principles in all our actions and decisions.

2. Gender Equality

We believe in equal rights and opportunities for women, challenging discrimination at every level.

3. Respect for Human Rights

We advocate for and protect the fundamental rights of every mother and child we serve.

4. Transparency & Accountability

We are open about our operations and accountable to our beneficiaries, partners, and the public.

5. Empathy & Compassion

We listen, understand, and act with deep care for the struggles faced by single mothers.

6. Community Participation

We ensure that our interventions are driven by the communities we aim to support.

7. Inclusiveness

We embrace diversity and ensure no mother or child is left behind due to background or circumstance.

Our Strategic Objectives

  • To economically empower vulnerable single mothers through vocational and entrepreneurship training.
  • To support children's education by providing school fees, uniforms, and scholastic materials.
  • To improve access to health, reproductive, and psychosocial support services.
  • To advocate for the rights and dignity of single mothers through legal aid and public engagement.
  • To reduce stigma and promote social reintegration of single mothers.
  • To strengthen the leadership and life skills of single mothers.
  • To establish partnerships that promote sustainable, long-term social impact.

A Message from Our Founder & Director

“I did not choose the circumstances that led me to raise my child as a single mother, but I chose not to let those circumstances define my future.”

My journey as a single mother is deeply connected to the reason Rise to Empower Single Mothers Africa (RESMA) exists. I understand, personally, the weight of raising a child without adequate support—the financial pressure, social stigma, uncertainty, and emotional challenges that many mothers silently carry.

For many women, single motherhood is not simply a personal choice or circumstance. It is often rooted in gender-based violence, abandonment, exploitation, unequal power relations, and social and economic inequalities that leave women carrying the responsibility of raising children alone. Behind the growing number of single-mother households are women whose lives have been profoundly affected by violence, discrimination, and the absence of meaningful protection and support.

This reality must not be met with judgment. It must be met with dignity, justice, protection, opportunity, and hope.

RESMA was born from this conviction. We believe that a single mother does not need pity—she needs an opportunity to rebuild, a safe environment in which to thrive, access to economic opportunities, healthcare and social support, and a voice to defend her rights.

Through economic empowerment, vocational skills, livelihood support, health services, child protection and education, human rights advocacy, and community-based interventions, RESMA addresses both the immediate needs and the underlying causes of vulnerability. We seek to help mothers become economically resilient while ensuring that their children remain safe, healthy, and in school.

I founded RESMA because I know what it means to face challenges that can make a woman feel alone. I also know the strength that exists within a mother when someone believes in her.

Our mission is therefore bigger than supporting individual mothers. We are working to break cycles of violence, poverty, discrimination, and dependency—while creating pathways for women and children to live with dignity and hope.

I invite partners, communities, institutions, and well-wishers to join us in this journey. Together, we can create a society where no woman is defined by the violence or hardship she has experienced, and where every child has the opportunity to grow, learn, and dream.

“When we empower a mother, we strengthen a family. When we protect a mother from violence, we protect a generation. And when we keep her child in school, we invest in the future of Africa.”

Katusabe Ruth

Director & Founder

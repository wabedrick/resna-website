Governance & Leadership

A transparent and accountable structure ensuring effective execution of our mission to empower single mothers and children.

A Message from Our Founder & Director

“As a single mother, I understand the struggles, hopes, and dreams of women raising children against the odds. RESMA was born from my belief that no mother should have to choose between feeding her children and giving them a better future.”

Through RESMA, we are creating opportunities for economic empowerment, health, education, and dignity for vulnerable mothers and their children.

When we empower a mother, we empower an entire family—and when we transform families, we transform communities.

Katusabe Ruth

Founder & Director, RESMA

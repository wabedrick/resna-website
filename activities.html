Our Programs

Practical, community-driven interventions that promote Economic empowerment, maternal dignity, health, and economic independence.

Flagship Initiative Motto: “We Rise Together”

Project: Empowering Vulnerable Single Mothers and Their Children

Vision: A defined, empowered, and self-sustaining life for all vulnerable single mothers and their children in Africa.

Target Beneficiaries

• Single Mothers: 18 – 35 Years
• Children: 3 – 17 Years

Project Duration

2 Years
Empowerment, Self-Reliance and Sustainable Livelihoods Programme

Location

Fort Portal Tourism City
Western Uganda

Transformative Pillars of Intervention

Practical, community-driven interventions designed to break poverty cycles and create long-term self-reliance.

💼 Economic Empowerment & Entrepreneurship Skills

Delivering comprehensive entrepreneurship skills training, business mentorship, seed grants, and financial literacy to launch profitable small businesses.

🛠️ Vocational Skilling & Hands-On Trades

Providing hands-on vocational workshops in tailoring, garment making, crafts, and agro-processing that yield immediate household income.

🏥 Health Services, Counseling & Social Education

Facilitating reproductive healthcare access, maternal wellness checks, psychosocial counseling and guidance, and community social education.

📚 Education Support & Scholastic Materials

Eliminating educational barriers by covering tuition fees and providing scholastic materials for every child to acquire formal education.

⚖️ Women's Rights, Children's Rights & Civic Education

Advocating for maternal and children's fundamental rights, defending property rights, providing legal aid, and delivering community civic education.

🌍 Income Generating Projects & Community Action

Establishing community-based income generating projects, village savings and loan associations (VSLA), and grassroots poverty eradication initiatives.

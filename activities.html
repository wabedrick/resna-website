Our Programs & Activities

Practical, community-driven interventions that promote empowerment, maternal dignity, health, and economic independence.

Transformative Pillars of Intervention

Every program is designed in close consultation with the single mothers we serve to ensure sustainability and measurable impact.

💼 Economic Empowerment & Livelihoods

Equipping single mothers with startup capital, seed grants, and financial literacy to launch and sustain profitable small businesses.

🛠️ Skills Development & Vocational Training

Providing hands-on training in tailoring, crafts, agro-processing, and marketable skills that yield immediate household income.

🏥 Health Services & Psychosocial Care

Facilitating access to reproductive healthcare, maternal checkups, mental wellness circles, and trauma counseling.

📚 Education & Child Support

Eliminating educational barriers by providing school fees, uniforms, and scholastic kits for children of single mothers.

🛡️ Child Protection & Family Welfare

Safeguarding children's fundamental rights, preventing domestic abuse, and establishing safe community spaces.

🌍 Community Outreach & Rights Advocacy

Working alongside local leadership to dismantle stigma, protect property rights, and champion gender equality.

Our Core Mission

Empowering Single Mothers Through Holistic Support

The main interventions of RESMA are rooted in social justice, vocational skilling, and child welfare. We provide vulnerable single mothers with the practical tools, financial literacy, and healthcare access required to break intergenerational poverty.

Through sustainable, inclusive, and community-driven initiatives, we support women and children facing difficult circumstances due to socio-economic exclusion, stigma, or loss.

Why We Exist, The Problem & Our Action

A clear, transparent breakdown of the core challenge we address, the current reality in our communities, and the concrete actions we take every day.

👩‍👧‍👦 Why Single Mothers?

The Catalyst

Mothers are the backbone of African families. When a single mother receives livelihood skills and healthcare access, over 90% of her earnings go directly back into feeding, clothing, and educating her children.

Multiplier Effect

Empowering 1 mother creates direct protection and schooling for an average of 4 children.

  • Breaks the cycle of generational poverty
  • Strengthens community economic resilience
  • Prevents early childhood vulnerability

⚠️ The Reality & Need

The Challenge

In vulnerable rural and peri-urban communities, single mothers face systemic isolation, lack of startup capital, and cultural discrimination, leaving their young children at severe risk of dropping out of school.

65%+ Vulnerability Rate

Over 65% of single-parent households lack stable income or access to basic medical insurance.

  • High primary-school dropout rates
  • Lack of collateral for traditional bank loans
  • Inadequate maternal and psychosocial care

⚡ Our Concrete Action

The Solution

We deliver hands-on vocational skilling, micro-grant starter kits, child school fees sponsorship, and human rights advocacy so mothers become financially independent and their children stay in school.

Holistic Grassroots Model

Direct skilling, legal aid, psychosocial counseling, and nutrition support.

  • Vocational training & small business startup grants
  • Child tuition, uniforms, & scholastic kits
  • Community support groups & mental wellness
